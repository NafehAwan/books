Tiny Motors privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 (car skins, play reminders)

Tiny Motors is a game for children aged 5 to 10. We (the developer) do not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal information about children or parents.

Game progress (saved on the device, backed up by Android)

The game board, scores, coins, unlocked cars, bought themes and car skins, settings, the date of the last daily gift, how many reward videos were watched today, whether the tutorial was seen and whether "Remove ads" was bought are saved on the device, so the child can continue where they left off.

So that progress and anything bought are not lost when the app is reinstalled or the family moves to a new phone, this one save file is included in Android's own backup. If backup is turned on in the phone's settings, Android stores it in the family's own Google account (on recent Android versions it is end-to-end encrypted when the phone has a screen lock) and puts it back when the app is installed again. We (the developer) cannot see or access these backups, and no other app data is included. Parents can turn backup off in the phone's settings (Settings → System → Backup, or Google → Backup, depending on the phone).

This information is never sent to us. It is deleted from the device when the app is uninstalled, when the app's storage is cleared, or with Settings → Parent Reset (hold for 3 seconds) inside the app; a Parent Reset is also carried into the next backup. Purchases are kept by Google Play.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK:

  • a small banner on the Home and Garage screens and in a strip above the game board (it never covers the game), and
  • optional reward videos that the child chooses to watch for coins (at most 5 a day). Every video is labelled "Ad".

Every ad request is marked child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR), and only G-rated ads are allowed. The app does not use the advertising ID (the permission is removed) and does not use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad signals. Because of these settings Google serves only non-personalised ads and limits the data it processes. To deliver ads and keep them safe, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address (to estimate a country), device and app information, and ad interactions. See Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431.

Parents can turn banners off for good with the optional Remove ads purchase.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Coin packs and "Remove ads" are optional purchases made through Google Play. Every purchase button sits behind a grown-up gate (press and hold for 3 seconds), and Google Play then asks for the account's own payment confirmation. Payment details are handled only by Google; the app never sees them. Coins can also be earned for free by playing.

Other

  • No accounts, no chat, no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No links that lead out of the app, apart from what an ad itself may open after a tap.
  • Car names are read aloud with the device's own text-to-speech engine, on the device.

Play reminders (notifications)

When the child leaves the game, it can show up to three friendly notes on the phone: a present is ready (the next day), what car comes next (3 days later) and how many mystery cars are still hiding (7 days later), each around 5 pm. Then nothing more is sent until the game is played again. These notes are made on the device from the saved game; nothing is sent to or from a server, and they never mention purchases or ads. On Android 13 and newer the phone asks first whether the app may show notifications. Parents can switch reminders off in Settings → General → Reminders or in the phone's notification settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer's contact email shown on the app's Google Play page. Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
