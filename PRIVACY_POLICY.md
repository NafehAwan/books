# Tiny Motors privacy policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Tiny Motors is a game for children. It does **not** collect, store on any server, share or sell
any personal or non-personal data.

* **No data collection.** The app does not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location,
  photos, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage statistics or crash reports.
* **No internet.** The app does not request the internet permission and cannot connect to any
  server.
* **No ads, analytics, accounts, purchases or third-party SDKs.** There are no links that lead
  out of the app.
* **No permissions.** The app asks for no Android permissions.
* **What stays on the device.** The game board, scores, unlocked cars, settings, the date of the
  last daily gift and whether the tutorial was seen are saved only on the device, so the child can
  continue where they left off. This information is not backed up or transferred to other devices.
  It is deleted when the app is uninstalled, when the app's storage is cleared, or with
  **Settings → For grown-ups → Hold to start over** inside the app.
* **Speech.** When "Say car names" is on, the app asks the device's built-in text-to-speech engine
  to read car names aloud. Only the car name is passed to the engine, on the device.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent to the developer's contact email shown on the
app's Google Play page.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
