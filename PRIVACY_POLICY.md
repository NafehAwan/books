# Tiny Motors privacy policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Tiny Motors is a game for children aged 5 to 10. We (the developer) do not collect, store on any
server, sell or share any personal information about children or parents.

## What stays on the device

The game board, scores, coins, unlocked cars, bought themes, settings, the date of the last daily
gift, how many reward videos were watched today, whether the tutorial was seen and whether "Remove
ads" was bought are saved **only on the device**, so the child can continue where they left off.
This information is never sent to us, is not backed up and is not copied to other devices. It is
deleted when the app is uninstalled, when the app's storage is cleared, or with
**Settings → Parent Reset** (hold for 3 seconds) inside the app. Purchases are kept by Google Play.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK:

* a small banner on the Home and Garage screens (never during play), and
* optional reward videos that the child chooses to watch for coins (at most 5 a day). Every video
  is labelled "Ad".

Every ad request is marked **child-directed** (COPPA) and **under the age of consent** (GDPR), and
only **G-rated** ads are allowed. The app does **not** use the advertising ID (the permission is
removed) and does not use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad signals. Because of these settings Google
serves only non-personalised ads and limits the data it processes. To deliver ads and keep them
safe, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address (to estimate a
country), device and app information, and ad interactions. See Google's policies:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and
<https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431>.

Parents can turn banners off for good with the optional **Remove ads** purchase.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Coin packs and "Remove ads" are optional purchases made through Google Play. Every purchase button
sits behind a grown-up gate (press and hold for 3 seconds), and Google Play then asks for the
account's own payment confirmation. Payment details are handled only by Google; the app never sees
them. Coins can also be earned for free by playing.

## Other

* No accounts, no chat, no location, no contacts, no camera, no microphone.
* No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
* No links that lead out of the app, apart from what an ad itself may open after a tap.
* Car names are read aloud with the device's own text-to-speech engine, on the device.

## Contact
